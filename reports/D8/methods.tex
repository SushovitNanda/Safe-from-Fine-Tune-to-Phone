\section{Judging, inference and interpretation}
English/Hinglish safety primary is Qwen3Guard Unsafe (Controversial safe primary, unsafe sensitivity). Hindi uses WildGuard after IndicTrans2 back-translation. Qwen3Guard refusal supplies ORR throughout; Hinglish-specific ORR is directional and pooled ORR retains that caveat. Two-sided percentile-bootstrap tail p-values follow the central lane, with floor 1/10000. Historical exact-test exploratory outputs remain preserved and labelled. The supplied 35 core and15 deployment tests retain their source scopes; eight prospectively named RQ6 comparisons extend deployment, and the complete conservative58-test union is reported as a sensitivity. English protected-minus-random survives the23-test deployment family but not that wider union; conclusions always identify the family. Registration timing limitations and omitted historical tests are not silently repaired by relabelling.

Repair gain is$G_s=A_{\mathrm{med},s}-A_{\mathrm{steer},s}$; retention$R(s)=G_s/G_{\mathrm{F16}}$ is available only when the observed Mac F16 gain is at least five points. All rates/gains accompany ratios. Shared resamples preserve covariance, below-floor/nonpositive denominators are counted and a ratio interval is withheld if nonpositive resamples occur rather than silently conditioning on their exclusion. A meaningful difference requires at least five points with an interval clear of zero; pointwise noninferiority requires the upper 95\% safety-loss bound below five points. Neither pointwise interval is a simultaneous adjusted claim. Prompt-level resampling does not automatically account for dependence among translation variants or clinical twins; a cluster sensitivity has not been established.

Same-engine difference-in-differences requires original Mac F16, which is absent. The provided laptop-reference calculation is a labelled mixed-engine sensitivity, not the primary within-Mac result. Phone-minus-Mac F16 (plan), no-preamble-minus-same-precision-official (handoff) and phone-minus-Q3 (combined stack) are separately named. The unsteered preamble contrast does not itself test H5's repair-retention prediction. All pooled retention intervals include1, so H5 is not supported; this is not proof of equivalence. A post-hoc central heuristic flags repetitions/echoes/short outputs, including legitimate short refusals; non-degenerate ASR is sensitivity only and never replaces the frozen primary labels.
