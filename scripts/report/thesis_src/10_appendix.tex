\subsection{Cost scenario assumptions}
The conditional cost scenario in Section~\ref{sec:rq5} assumes 10,000 requests a month at the measured Q3\_K\_M token means (391 input, 127 output), a 600 USD machine amortised over 36 months at 50\,\% allocation, 30 available hours a month at 50\,\% utilisation, 40 W at 0.20 USD per kWh, 5 USD of other monthly cost, and a listed API price of 0.40 USD per million input and 1.60 USD per million output tokens (GPT-4.1 mini list price checked on 11 November 2026, marked deprecated at the source). Result: 13.6 USD a month local against 3.6 USD API; capacity about 32,600 requests a month; break-even about 37,700. Sensitivity runs at 1,000 requests and 20 to 60 W do not change the sign. Quality equivalence between the local model and the API model was not measured.
